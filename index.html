About FSC Team

FSC Motorsport is a Formula 3 racing team competing in the Japanese F3 National Championship. We are proudly powered by Toyota Gazoo Racing — TOM'S.

FSC stands for “French Speaking Connection,” founded by driver-owner Guillaume Cunnington and Antoine Malin. We operate the team in Japan. Our rear wing, labeled #FrenchTouch, became our trademark within the local fans.

Guillaume Cunnington is the champion of the Asian Formula Renault Masters and has also entered the Macau Grand Prix.
